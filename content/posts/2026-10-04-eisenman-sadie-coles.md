What other painter alive has [Nicole Eisenman](https://artangled.com/tags/nicole-eisenman/)’s virtuosic skill _and_ range of ambition? This [new show at Sadie Coles](https://www.sadiecoles.com/exhibitions/nicole-eisenman-2) includes metallic and kinetic sculptures as well as a dizzying variety of paintings.

Downstairs in the gallery, there are two grand canvases downstairs, huge skies full of stars - and drolly reflected by the meticulous recreation, in one of the works, of an old MacBook with the MacOS galaxy wallpaper on its minutely detailed screen.

Better still, upstairs, there’s _The Orgy_ (pictured below). A sumptuous six panel work that’s a tangle of disembodied limbs and different painted textures - so different. Thrillingly compositionally coherent, Eisenman pulls focus in the bottom left hand corner with a burly fist, gripping the white sheets.

![Nicole Eisenman at Sadie Coles HQ. l-r ‘The Orgy’ (2026), ‘Togetherers’ (2026)](/eisenman-sadie-coles-1.jpeg)
_l-r ‘The Orgy’ (2026), ‘Togetherers’ (2026)_

Finally, in the last room, a Philip Guston tribute featuring a clown car full of ICE and a monster emerging from its oily exhaust. Both themes in the [first Eisenman show I saw](https://artangled.com/posts/eisenman-secession/), at Secession in Vienna in 2017.

While all these styles reference things I saw at Eisenman’s large big UK show, [at Whitechapel Gallery in 2023](https://artangled.com/posts/eisenman-whitechapel/), down to the oil painted Apple devices, the works on show represent a step forward. All of them were painted this year, by an artist now in her 60s. All of them breathtakingly vital. All so different.

The answer to the question I started the post with - for me at least - is nobody.

_Nicole Eisenman is at Sadie Coles HQ (London). 23 September - 21 November 2026_
